# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- Charmed Mana Beasts (lamas) lose their charm once they are inside the herding pen, so they no longer follow you back
  out. Host-side; no game patches.
- A decharmed lama walks a bit further into the pen (to a spot nearer the center) instead of stopping at the edge.
- Test hotkeys (`Debug.TestHotkeys`): Ctrl+1 starts a herding objective, Ctrl+2 charms its lamas outside the pen.
