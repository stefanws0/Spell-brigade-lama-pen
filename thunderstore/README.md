# Lama Pen

In the herding objective, charmed lamas follow you around. That's handy until you've led one into the pen and it
follows you straight back out.

With this mod, a charmed lama loses its charm as soon as it's inside the pen. It walks a few steps toward the middle
and then stays in the pen like any other lama.

Only the host needs the mod, because the host's game moves the lamas. I haven't tested it in co-op yet.

## Settings

In `BepInEx/config/com.stefanws0.spellbrigadelamapen.cfg`, created on first launch:

- `Enabled`: the mod on or off
- `LogLamas`: log what every lama does, handy for bug reports
- `TestHotkeys`: Ctrl+1 starts a herding objective and Ctrl+2 charms its lamas, for testing (off by default)

Source and bug reports: https://github.com/stefanws0/Spell-brigade-lama-pen
